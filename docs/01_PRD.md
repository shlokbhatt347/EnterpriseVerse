# EnterpriseVerse Product Requirements

## Vision

Build a practical entrepreneurship simulator in which the learner develops entrepreneurial judgment by running a business in a persistent, reactive market.

## Core loop

Observe → decide → consequence → reflect → adapt.

## Business structures

- Sole trader: maximum control and speed, concentrated responsibility and risk.
- Partnership: shared ownership, complementary skills, coordination and conflict trade-offs.
- Trio: role specialization with more coordination overhead.
- Team/company: departments, delegation, voting, governance and organizational complexity.

## Worlds

Practice worlds use AI-run enterprises. Multiplayer worlds contain real student enterprises in a shared economy. Classroom worlds allow a teacher to configure events and monitor learning.

## Practical skills

Opportunity recognition, customer discovery, selling, negotiation, pricing, budgeting, cash management, operations, hiring, leadership, marketing, risk management, communication, collaboration, adaptation and reflection.

## Design rule

The simulator should not require a student to study theory before acting. Concepts appear as they become useful to solve a realistic business problem.

## Success

Success is not simply maximum profit. The product measures decision quality, business health, relationships, resilience, experimentation and sustainable growth.
