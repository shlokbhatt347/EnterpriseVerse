# Phase 11 — Advanced Living World

## Objective
Deepen the existing customer, supplier, competitor, investor, workforce, market, consequence and scenario systems into a deterministic world layer without duplicating domain logic.

## Workstreams
- Autonomous actor decisions
- Customer relationship and churn dynamics
- Supplier reliability and relationship dynamics
- Competitor strategy and market response
- Workforce morale, productivity and turnover
- Market evolution and pressure
- Delayed cross-system consequences
- Actor memory and relationship history
- Deterministic seeded runs and long-horizon regression tests

## Release gate
Phase 11 is complete only after simulation tests, typecheck, web build and CI are green, with long-run deterministic simulations and state-integrity tests passing.
