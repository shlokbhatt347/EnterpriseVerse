# Phase 4 — Living Enterprise Simulation + Top-Tier Executive Command Center

Phase 4 extends the existing Phase 19–26 living-world engine rather than creating a second simulation.

## Runtime contract

`SimulationState` remains canonical. Phase 4 derives an executive read model and applies changes through existing simulation transitions.

## New capabilities

- Causal market pulse: demand, pricing, customer confidence and competitor pressure from the current state.
- Workforce health: morale, productivity, payroll and turnover risk from the existing workforce model.
- Supply-chain risk from inventory/stockout state.
- Deterministic state-driven attention signals for cash, competition, demand, supply, workforce and reputation.
- Executive briefing: what is happening, why it is happening, and what deserves attention.
- Decision preview: estimate cash, revenue, health and risk deltas before committing a choice.
- Bounded competitive intelligence and world feed.
- Premium executive command-center UI with KPI strip, attention center, market pulse, decision room, priorities, company health and market leaders.
- Mobile-first responsive layout and reduced-motion support.

## Performance

No polling or per-frame simulation is introduced. The command center is a pure derived view of the current immutable `SimulationState`, so the simulation advances only when the player advances/commits a decision.

## Realism

Attention signals are generated from real simulation conditions and the decision preview is generated by projecting the same canonical simulation choice model rather than inventing a parallel forecast system.
