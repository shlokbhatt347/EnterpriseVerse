# EnterpriseVerse

> Build businesses. Not just knowledge.

EnterpriseVerse is an AI-powered entrepreneurship simulator where students learn practical entrepreneurship by running businesses in a living market.

## Vision

Students choose a business structure, create a company, serve customers, negotiate with suppliers, manage money and inventory, lead people, compete with AI and student-run enterprises, and learn from consequences.

## Monorepo

- `apps/web` — Next.js client
- `apps/api` — FastAPI service
- `packages/simulation` — framework-independent simulation engine
- `packages/types` — shared domain contracts
- `docs` — product and technical specifications

## Principles

1. Consequences over lectures.
2. Decisions have trade-offs.
3. AI characters remember relationships and history.
4. The market evolves continuously.
5. Multiplayer businesses share an economy.
6. Feedback improves judgment instead of replacing it.

## Simulation validation

Phase 3 long-horizon, deterministic world behavior is covered by the simulation quality gate.

Final Phase 3 branch validation is required before merge.
